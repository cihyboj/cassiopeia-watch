Cassiopeia Watch is an unofficial, open-source Android app built and tested for the Casio ABL-100WE Bluetooth watch. Other Casio Bluetooth watches may work but are untested. It works without a Casio account. It is a derivative of CasioGShockSmartSync by Ivo Zivkov and is not affiliated with Casio.

Experimental prerelease. Only the ABL-100WE has been tested by the maintainer.

Features
- Send the phone time, time zone and DST setting to the watch, optionally using an NTP time source
- Read and edit watch alarms and the hourly signal
- Send phone calendar events to the watch as reminders (watches that support reminders)
- Choose what a short or long watch button press does: send a broadcast intent for automation apps, or ring the phone
- Pair more than one watch; local sync history
- Export and import app settings

The app has no accounts, analytics or ads, and no server of its own. The only network traffic is optional NTP time queries.

Source code, issues and privacy policy: https://github.com/cihyboj/cassiopeia-watch
